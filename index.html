Private Jet Travel: The Insider's Guide

JUNE 10, 2026 · LUXURY TRAVEL

Flying private is no longer reserved for billionaires and A-list celebrities. The industry has shifted dramatically in recent years, with empty-leg flights, jet card memberships, and on-demand charter apps making private aviation more accessible than ever. But knowing how to navigate the options is key to getting real value.

The most cost-effective entry point is empty-leg bookings — one-way flights at up to 75% off when a jet needs to reposition. Apps like TimeFlys aggregate these deals in real time. For frequent travelers, jet cards offer guaranteed availability at fixed hourly rates. And for the ultimate flexibility, on-demand charter lets you book exactly what you need, when you need it. The golden rule: always compare at least three quotes and book with operators that have Wyvern or ARGUS safety certification.

Our pick: for European travel, TimeFlys consistently offers the best balance of pricing and aircraft quality. We've used them for Amalfi, Ibiza, and Saint-Tropez routes with flawless results.

Why Every Trip Needs a Concierge

MAY 28, 2026 · TRAVEL TIPS

The difference between a good trip and an extraordinary one often comes down to what happens behind the scenes. A travel concierge doesn't just book things — they orchestrate. They know which hotel room has the best view, which restaurant will squeeze you in when every table is booked, and which transfer company actually arrives early rather than late.

The value is most apparent when something goes wrong. A cancelled flight, a missed connection, a hotel that's overbooked — these moments separate standard service from exceptional support. A good concierge has relationships with airlines, hotel managers, and local operators. A call from them carries weight that a call from you simply doesn't. They can rebook, re-route, or upgrade you in minutes rather than hours.

For the price of one upgrade, you can have a concierge manage your entire trip. The peace of mind alone is worth it. The real magic, though, is the access — tables, tours, and experiences that never appear on public booking platforms.

Beyond Positano: Hidden Gems of the Amalfi

JUNE 18, 2026 · DESTINATIONS

Everyone knows Positano, Amalfi, and Ravello. But the Amalfi Coast has a quieter side that rewards those who venture beyond the famous three. Take Atrani — a picture-perfect fishing village just a five-minute walk from Piazza Duomo in Amalfi, yet completely overlooked by the crowds. Its piazza comes alive in the evening with locals playing cards and children kicking footballs, while tourists stream past unaware.

Further east, Cetara is a working fishing port that hasn't yet been polished for tourism. This is where you come for the best colatura di alici (anchovy sauce) on the coast, at trattorias where fishermen eat. And for a true escape, the tiny hamlet of Conca dei Marini has secluded pebble beaches accessible only by boat or a steep staircase — the kind of place where you can swim in silence with nothing but the sound of waves against limestone cliffs. These are the spots that stay with you long after the photos of Positano have faded from your feed.
