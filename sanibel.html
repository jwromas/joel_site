Sanibel Island

Paradise Awaits — 15 Miles of Unspoiled Gulf Coast

Why Sanibel

One of America's top shelling destinations. Crystal-clear Gulf waters, world-class fishing, and nature preserves make it the ultimate escape.

🐚

World-Class Shelling

15 miles of pristine shoreline — Sanibel is famous for its abundance of rare shells washed ashore by unique east-west orientation.

🌊

Crystal Gulf Waters

Warm, calm, turquoise waters ideal for swimming, paddleboarding, and watching dolphins play at sunset.

🦅

Nature Preserves

J.N. "Ding" Darling National Wildlife Refuge — 6,400 acres of mangroves, kayaking trails, and rare bird sightings.

🏨

Boutique Island Inns

Unlike big-chain beaches, Sanibel's charm lies in independently owned resorts and inns — many beachfront, all with personality.

Things to Do

From secluded beaches to sunset yacht cruises — Sanibel has it all.

Sanibel Island Info

Everything you need to know before you go.

Getting There

• Airport: Southwest Florida Intl (RSW) — 30 min drive

• Best time: November–April (dry season, 75–85°F)

• Currency: USD

• Transport: Car recommended — bridge toll to island

Travel Tips

• Shell collecting is legal on Sanibel — but only empty shells

• Sunset spot: Bowman's Beach or Captiva's Chapel by the Sea

• Wildlife: Manatees, dolphins, and nesting sea turtles (May–Oct)

• Tip: Book yacht charters early — they fill up fast in peak season
